# Alien art specification and G5m quality gate

## Required creature

- Elongated, smooth, eyeless cranial dome.
- Forward-leaning head/neck, hunched predatory posture.
- Near-black charcoal/gunmetal biomechanical exoskeleton.
- Ribbed torso, narrow waist, long articulated limbs.
- Arms lowered, reaching or preparing to strike; never a symmetrical V.
- Long clawed hands, powerful digitigrade legs.
- Long segmented tail with subtle directional highlights.
- Consistent anatomy across idle, walk1, walk2, attack, side and death.
- Recognisable silhouette at actual in-game/distant size.

## Prohibited

- Flower/insect symmetry, bright green cartoon body, glowing eyes.
- Stick-figure limbs, oversized exposed teeth, flat geometric diagrams.
- Recolours/rotations/scales of the failed sprite presented as new artwork.
- Proprietary Jaguar/Alien game frames or extracted studio artwork.

## G5m assessment

The current 128×192 procedural set is technically valid: upright, transparent,
six frames, consistent pivot/baseline, readable at distance. It remains below
the final art gate: the head/limb construction still reads as stylised
mechanical geometry rather than a coherent biomechanical predator. No new
replacement is claimed. A separately produced artwork pipeline (model/sculpt or
carefully painted high-resolution frames) is required for final acceptance.

Renderer/gameplay integration should continue to use the six current frames
until replacement artwork passes this gate.
