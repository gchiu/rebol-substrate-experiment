# G5x2 — Native-Resolution Blast-Door Leaves

**Status: implemented in OpenGL; committed locally, not pushed.**

G5x2 replaces the G5x door-leaf crops with the two supplied 1070×1470
high-resolution industrial door images. Geometry, casing, pockets, animation,
collision thresholds, Alien navigation and Glon semantics are unchanged.

**Branch/worktree:** `glon-avp-g5x2` at
`/mnt/d/repos/glon-worktrees/glon-avp-g5x2`.
**Starting point:** G5x `174b9ebdce437b3dd0900d6cbe74a1223e31a7fd`.
**G5x2 implementation:** `24c586039636d9a8536aec322584942f47b17793`.
**G5x2 evidence/report:** this commit.

No push, merge, tag or deploy was performed. Frozen S1 is unchanged.

## 2. Crop and seam alignment

The existing leaf face is 64 world units wide × 112 high (aspect 0.5714); the
source is 0.728. Stretching would distort the art, so the converter keeps the
full 1470-pixel height and crops horizontally to the face aspect:

| Leaf | Crop box | Runtime size | Raw RGB |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| left | `(230, 0, 1070, 1470)` | 840×1470 | 3,704,400 B |
| right | `(0, 0, 840, 1470)` | 840×1470 | 3,704,400 B |

The left leaf keeps its right (seam) edge; the right leaf keeps its left (seam)
edge. The 230-pixel trim is entirely on the outer edge. No upscaling,
retouching, mirroring or procedural reconstruction is used. The independently
generated leaves have different panel layouts by design; this is not a mirror
error.

Door texture wrapping is `GL_CLAMP_TO_EDGE`. The quads span exactly 0..1, and
clamping prevents the inner seam edge from blending with the outer edge under
the renderer's previous `GL_REPEAT` setting.

## 3. Resolution and sampling

Both leaves are **13.125 texels per world unit** horizontally and vertically
(840/64 and 1470/112). The G5x leaves were about 7.0 texels per unit (left
450/64 = 7.03; right 410/64 = 6.41; both 785/112 = 7.01).

Sampling remains the renderer's established fixed-function `GL_LINEAR`
(magnification and minification). Mipmapped sampling was tested and rejected:
both `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` and `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_NEAREST` raised per-frame
cost on this WSLg D3D12 driver (object-mode median about 55–59 FPS versus 65
FPS, with tick time rising from about 8.9 ms to about 11.5 ms). No mipmaps are
used, matching the rest of this renderer.

Texture memory changes by **+7,178,000 bytes**:

| Set | GPU RGBA bytes |
|---|---:|
| G5x (450×785 + 410×785) | 2,700,400 |
| G5x2 (840×1470 × 2) | 9,878,400 |

The assets are loaded once from raw RGB at startup. They are not embedded in
the executable; the executable remains **6,121,456 bytes**, the same as G5x.

## 4. Preserved behaviour

- Side pockets, jambs, header, threshold, fasteners, warning lamps and the

## 7. Measurements

Randomized, interleaved old/new A/B, 180 frames, 960×600, WSLg D3D12 AMD
Radeon. Six runs per configuration; cooldown between runs:

| Mode | G5x old median | G5x2 median | Old range | G5x2 range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No objects | 98.2 FPS | 106.9 FPS | 84.8–108.6 | 85.0–108.1 |
| With objects | 67.0 FPS | 65.2 FPS | 66.4–70.2 | 57.9–68.2 |

Tick time was effectively unchanged (median 3.67 → 3.74 ms no objects;
8.86 → 9.04 ms with objects). The object-mode difference is about **-2.7%**
at the median but within the observed spread. A separate sequential set
measured 64.6 → 62.8 FPS object-mode; the randomized paired set is the
authoritative comparison. In short: no systematic tick or framing regression
was found, but the larger texture does not produce a measurable speed-up
either.

A controlled mipmap experiment is the one clear cost: mipmapping the leaves
reduced object-mode median FPS to about 55–59 and raised tick time by about
2.6 ms. That is why mipmaps are not used.

## 8. Visual assessment

**Measured:** texel density rises from about 7.0 to 13.125 texels per world
unit, and both runtime leaves are 840×1470 instead of 450×785/410×785.

**Subjective:** the 24-unit and 1920 close-ups show clearly resolved bolts,
paint wear, vents and panel edges instead of the previous soft blocks. The
seam edges meet cleanly, partially open states show the same leaf edges and
thickness as G5x, and the fully open leaves still park behind the existing
pocket covers. No visible gap, clipping, mirroring or UV rotation was found in
the captured poses.

## 9. Limitations

- The aspect-preserving crop removes the outer 230 pixels of each source,
  including the left leaf's outer vent and the right leaf's baked control strip.
  The supplied README says the control strip is not a functional control; the
  3D lock/status housing remains.
- At 1920 the extreme close-up still shows some source softness because the
  840-pixel leaf covers roughly 1500 screen pixels. No fake upscale was used.
- Without mipmaps, distant minification can still shimmer in motion; this was
  not measured as a still-image or gameplay regression, but it is the known
  trade-off of the renderer's GL_LINEAR path.
- The CPU fallback renderer still uses the old 64×64 `tex_door`; G5x2 is an
  OpenGL-path upgrade.
- Audio remains deferred; no CPU fallback texture upgrade was made.
