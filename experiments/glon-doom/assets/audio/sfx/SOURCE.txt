G6b event sound sources and licences
====================================

All five recordings are used under the licences shown on their Freesound
pages. They were converted to the game's mixer format (44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono
PCM WAV); door/rifle/wall/cargo clips were also trimmed and given short edge
fades to fit gameplay events. No synthesis was substituted.

1. Blast door: "Lab Door Open and Close Industrial Sci-Fi Games"
   Creator: kentspublicdomain (Kent)
   https://freesound.org/people/kentspublicdomain/sounds/799198/
   Licence: Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0), public domain
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Source: 24-bit mono 44.1 kHz, 6.000 s.
   Modifications: split into open (0.0-2.2 s) and close (2.2-4.4 s)
   excerpts; 5 ms in / 30 ms out fades; converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono.

2. Alien shriek: "Alien Monster Roaring and Screaming"
   Creator: Mastersoundboy2005
   https://freesound.org/people/mastersoundboy2005/sounds/737275/
   Licence: Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0), public domain
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Source: 16-bit stereo 48 kHz, 2.1667 s.
   Modifications: trimmed 50 ms leading silence, converted to 16-bit mono
   44.1 kHz; short edge fades.

3. Rifle: "Sci-Fi LMG Concept"
   Creator: Heretic4522
   https://freesound.org/people/Heretic4522/sounds/850829/
   Licence: Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0), public domain
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Source: 32-bit mono 48 kHz, 1.8047 s, described by the creator as one
   gunshot for a light machine gun.
   Modifications: extracted the single-shot 0.65 s attack/early tail with a
   30 ms fade-out; converted to 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz.

4. Wall impact: "Low Metal Thud 2.wav"
   Creator: F.M.Audio
   https://freesound.org/s/620830/
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Source: 24-bit mono 96 kHz, 3.9631 s.
   Modifications: trimmed to 1.2 s with a 50 ms fade-out; converted to
   16-bit mono 44.1 kHz.
   Attribution: "Low Metal Thud 2.wav" by F.M.Audio, CC BY 4.0.

5. Cargo impact: "Metal Impact"
   Creator: Rickplayer
   https://freesound.org/people/Rickplayer/sounds/530486/
   Licence: Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0), public domain
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Source: MP3 mono 44.1 kHz, 1.790 s.
   Modifications: decoded; 60 ms leading silence trimmed; 0.84 s excerpt
   with a 40 ms fade-out; converted to 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz.
