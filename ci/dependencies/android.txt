Target: Android ARM64 / Bionic, tested for Termux delivery.
Toolchain: Android NDK clang, aarch64-linux-android24, -fPIE -pie.
Runtime: Android Bionic libc/libm/libdl and pthread; no glibc, WSL, APK or
Android Studio project required for the command-line test build.
Capabilities: minimal and file-enabled Glon, JSON vocabulary, S1/M1 diagnostic
suite, examples. HTTPS is not included in D1 because Android TLS packaging was
not verified.
The Android ARM64 artifact is provisional until executed on a device. The
equivalent x86_64 Android build was used for emulator verification.
